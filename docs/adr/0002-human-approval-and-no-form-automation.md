# Aaron approves every application; the agent never fills web forms

No application is submitted without Aaron's explicit Approval. For Email applications the agent sends after Approval; for web-form applications (most employer applicant-tracking systems) it produces a Packet and Aaron submits it by hand. Filling third-party forms with a browser bot was rejected: no ATS offers a candidate-side apply API, bot form-filling sits in a grey area of their terms, and a bad automated submission can't be taken back.

## Consequences

- "Applied" in the success metric includes Packets Aaron submits by hand (~2 minutes each).
- Full autonomy may be reconsidered for Email applications once the agent's judgment has earned trust.
